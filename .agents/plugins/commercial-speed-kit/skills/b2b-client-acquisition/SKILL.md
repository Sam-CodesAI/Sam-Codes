# B2B Client Acquisition Runbook

## 1. Discovery
- Scan r/forhire, r/freelance_forhire, r/Hiring, r/SaaS via RSS or Playwright.
- Filter for posts with [Hiring] tag, explicit budget mentions, and technical problem statements.

## 2. The Instant Prototype Formula
- Never send a generic cover letter.
- Build a 3-minute working prototype or mockup before messaging.
- Host preview on Vercel or provide direct sample output.

## 3. The Inbound Flip (Public Comment Delivery - Zero Outbound DMs)
- The user's account CANNOT send outbound DMs/chats on Reddit ("You need a more established account to send chat invites") and X DMs are paywalled.
- Post the complete working solution / sample output directly as a public comment on the hiring thread.
- Conclude the comment with an invitation for the poster to initiate a chat with you or contact you on Telegram (`@Samarth1306`).
- Offer 24-48 hour delivery with zero risk guarantee.
